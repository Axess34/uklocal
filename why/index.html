Why a website?

Why your business needs a website

(and how Google brings you customers)

Facebook is great for keeping in touch with regulars. But when a pipe bursts at 8pm, or someone wants a good café for brunch, most people don’t search Facebook. They go to Google and Google Maps.

This is how your next customer looks for you

🔧“emergency plumber Manchester”

8pm, water everywhere, phone in hand.

☕“best brunch café Bristol”

Saturday morning, looking for somewhere new.

How Google finds you: 3 simple steps

  1. 1

    🔎Google finds your website

    Think of Google as a digital librarian. Its bots, called crawlers, scan the web for pages. When your Google Business Profile links to your own website, Google can read your services, location and opening hours straight from it.

  2. 2

    🗂️Google adds your pages to its index

    It’s like adding your card to a local directory, but with a card for everything you do. Without a website, Google only knows the basics. With one, each service or item on your menu can be found, for example “emergency plumber in Manchester” or “gluten-free brunch in Bristol”.

  3. 3

    📍You show up on Google Maps

    When people search nearby, Google often shows a map with a short list of local businesses (sometimes called the “Local Pack”). Your free Google Business Profile gives customers directions, a tap-to-call button and your star ratings. Your website adds the full picture: photos, your menu or services, prices and opening hours, so people who check you out before calling feel confident choosing you.

The honest bit: nobody can guarantee a top spot on Google. A website helps Google understand and trust your business, and helps you show up more often and stand out from businesses that only have a Facebook page.
